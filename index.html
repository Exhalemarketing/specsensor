OEM replacement sensors we source

Four core categories. If it has a nameplate and a part number, send it.

Vibration & predictive-maintenance sensors

Industrial accelerometers, velocity transmitters (4-20 mA loop-powered), proximity probes and wireless condition-monitoring nodes. Match by part number, sensitivity (mV/g), frequency range, connector and mounting.

Fixed gas & VOC transmitters

Toxic, combustible (catalytic / IR), O₂ and PID VOC transmitters. Match by gas, range, sensor technology, output (4-20 mA, HART, Modbus RTU/TCP, relays) and hazardous-area rating.

Refrigerant leak-detection modules

Monitors and sensor modules for R-744 (CO₂), ammonia (R-717), HFC/HFO (R-410A, R-32, R-454B, R-1234ze) and A2L systems. Match by refrigerant, ppm range, alarm set-points and output.

OEM replacement sensor heads & cells

Plug-in sensor heads, smart sensor modules and replacement cells for existing transmitters – matched to the host transmitter model so calibration and fit stay right.

Use cases & FAQ

Use case: a vibration transmitter failed on a critical pump – can you match it by part number?

Yes. Send the OEM part number from the nameplate (e.g. a loop-powered 4-20 mA velocity transmitter or an IEPE accelerometer). We confirm the exact OEM unit or a spec-equivalent with matching sensitivity, range, connector and mounting thread before quoting.

Use case: our fixed H₂S / combustible / VOC transmitter needs a like-for-like replacement in a Class I Div 1 area.

List the gas, range, output (4-20 mA, HART, Modbus) and the certification required (UL/CSA Class I Div 1, ATEX, IECEx, SIL). We only quote units whose hazardous-area rating the supplier confirms matches your requirement.

Use case: a refrigerant leak-detection module in a cold-storage or A2L HVAC system must be replaced.

Tell us the refrigerant (e.g. R-744 CO₂, R-717 ammonia, R-410A, R-32, R-454B), ppm range, alarm set-points and output, plus the existing model. We source the matching module or the compatible replacement sensor head.

Do you hold stock? Who ships the part?

No inventory. We source each request from authorized or specialist industrial suppliers, apply our service margin, and the supplier ships direct to your delivery location. Pricing, availability and technical suitability are confirmed with the supplier before any order.

I don't have the part number – can you still help?

Yes – give the manufacturer, sensor type, measurement range, output and certifications, and add photos of the nameplate in the notes (a shared link works). The more exact the specs, the faster and more accurate the match.
